## What I've worked on

- Interned on an **end-to-end financial intelligence platform** for A-share listed companies: data pipelines from raw filings to structured storage and vectorization, plus a retrieval-augmented (RAG) question-answering system that returns answers with verifiable source evidence instead of just a plausible-sounding guess.
- Interned in **investment analysis** at a fund in Beijing, working with SQL and Excel across 10,000+ record datasets to speed up reporting, and writing an investment report on the study-abroad industry.
- Ran a **50-experiment ablation study** on preprocessing strategies and classifiers for a news text classification task — the more interesting finding wasn't the accuracy numbers, it was that a throwaway metadata field (which outlet a story came from) sometimes predicted the label better than the article text itself.
- Teaching assistant for NYU's **Data Structures** course for the past year: weekly office hours, grading 20+ assignments on linked lists, stacks, trees, and hash tables, and helping students debug their way through recursion.
